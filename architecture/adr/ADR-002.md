# ADR-002: Workspace is the Boundary of Consistency

## Purpose

Define the boundary within which constitutional state is assessed as coherent.

## Context

Semantic integrity requires related constitutional elements to be evaluated together rather than as unrelated fragments.

## Decision

Workspace is the boundary of consistency. A conformance assessment SHALL consider the complete Workspace, its Domains, and their declared relationships.

## Alternatives

Per-Domain consistency was rejected because it cannot establish integrity for relationships spanning Domains.

## Consequences

Reviews and releases must identify the Workspace under consideration. A locally valid change may still require review when it affects Workspace-wide relationships or invariants.
