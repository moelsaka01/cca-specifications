# ADR-004: Persistence Preserves Workspace State

## Purpose

Define the constitutional role of persistence in relation to Workspace state.

## Context

Workspace state must remain durable across disposable Runtime lifetimes while preserving semantic identity and integrity.

## Decision

Persistence preserves Workspace state. It records and restores state without becoming a Domain, Service, Policy, Provider, or Contract, and without redefining Workspace structure.

## Alternatives

Treating persistence as an architectural owner was rejected because ownership belongs to Workspace and its governed elements.

## Consequences

Persistence is subordinate to Workspace semantics. Preservation operations are subject to the same consistency and review boundaries as the state they preserve.
