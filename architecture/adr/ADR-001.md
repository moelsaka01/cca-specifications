# ADR-001: Workspace is the Root Aggregate

## Purpose

Establish the constitutional root for all CCA structure and governance.

## Context

CCA requires one durable boundary within which Domains, Assets, Services, Policies, Providers, and Contracts are organized and reviewed.

## Decision

Workspace is the root aggregate. Every constitutional element belongs to exactly one Workspace. Workspace establishes identity, scope, and the authoritative boundary for structural relationships.

## Alternatives

No alternative is adopted. A hierarchy without a single root would leave ownership and review scope ambiguous.

## Consequences

Architecture review, compatibility assessment, and release governance are scoped to a Workspace. Cross-Workspace relationships are not constitutional structure.
