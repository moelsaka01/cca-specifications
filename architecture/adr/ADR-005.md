# ADR-005: Structural, Behavioral and Evolution Views

## Purpose

Define the approved views for describing and reviewing the architecture.

## Context

One view cannot adequately express organization, behavior, and controlled change while retaining a common vocabulary.

## Decision

CCA uses three complementary views:

- Structural view describes Workspace, Domains, Assets, Services, Policies, Providers, Contracts, and their relationships.
- Behavioral view describes service behavior and policy constraints within the structural boundary.
- Evolution view describes approved change, compatibility, and constitutional revision over time.

## Alternatives

An undifferentiated description was rejected because it obscures which claim concerns structure, behavior, or change.

## Consequences

Architecture reviews SHALL identify the applicable view or views. The views are complementary and SHALL use the same constitutional vocabulary.
