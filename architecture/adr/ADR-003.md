# ADR-003: Runtime is Disposable

## Purpose

State the lifetime rule for execution activity.

## Context

Execution activity must not redefine or become the durable owner of Workspace state.

## Decision

Runtime is disposable. Runtime may perform service behavior for a Workspace, but its lifetime does not establish, transfer, or own persistent Workspace state.

## Alternatives

Making Runtime durable was rejected because it would conflate execution lifetime with semantic ownership.

## Consequences

Runtime may be created, stopped, and replaced without changing the constitutional identity of the Workspace. Services remain behavior-oriented and Providers remain infrastructure-oriented.
